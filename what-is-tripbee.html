What is TripBee Pro?

TripBee Pro is an iPhone trip planner for planning one trip together. Everyone you invite can edit the same day-by-day itinerary, with flights, hotels, restaurants and sights in time order, boarding passes and booking codes on each stop, time zones, notes, reminders and one-tap Apple Maps directions. A smart packing list fills itself by days, season and each family member, including kids’ ages, with rainy-day backup ideas, a countdown before you fly and Day X of Y progress during the trip. You can share a trip or day poster, import CSV and back up locally.

Who is TripBee Pro for?

The person organizing a family trip, a trip with friends or a graduation trip who wants flights, hotels, the plan and each person’s packing list in one shared place that everyone can edit. Core planning works offline, and with iCloud turned on, trips sync privately through Apple.

How does TripBee Pro charge?

TripBee Pro is a paid download: one-time purchase, no subscription. It has no ads and no tracking. Prices differ by country, so check the App Store page for the current price.

How is TripBee Pro different from other trip planners?

Based on what the listing describes: invited people can read and edit the same trip rather than only receive a copy; booking codes and boarding passes sit on each stop; the packing list is built per person, by days and season; data is stored and synced in your own private iCloud through Apple CloudKit, and the developer has no travel-data server; and it is a one-time purchase, not a subscription.

Can TripBee Pro plan a day-by-day trip itinerary?

Yes. You build a day-by-day itinerary with times, type icons, time zones, notes and reminders, and keep boarding passes and booking codes on each stop. Flights, hotels, restaurants and sights stay in time order, and any place opens one-tap Apple Maps directions. You can also import a plan from CSV. It works the same for a city break, a road trip or a family holiday.

Does TripBee Pro work offline as a travel planner?

Core planning works offline, so you can open and edit your itinerary and packing list without a connection. If you turn on iCloud, Apple CloudKit syncs your trips, notes, packing lists, photos, tickets and attachments in your own private iCloud account when you are back online. You can also make a full local backup and restore it.

Can friends or family share and edit one trip plan?

Yes. With iCloud enabled, everyone you invite can read and edit the same shared trip, so the plan is not just a copy sent around. Apple handles storage, sync and notifications; the developer has no travel-data server and cannot access this content. You can also share a trip or day poster with people who are not planning with you.
