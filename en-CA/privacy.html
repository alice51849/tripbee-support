Privacy Policy

TripBee Pro: Trip Planner

How TripBee Pro: Trip Planner handles information, where eligible data stays, and which choices remain under your control.

Last updated: 2026-08-29

Data we collect

None. TripBee Pro: Trip Planner does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal data. There is no developer-run account or sign-in system.

iCloud and device sync

If you enable iCloud, Apple may store or synchronise eligible app data through your own iCloud account. Apple handles that service; the developer cannot read the synced content.

Sharing and exports

Sharing or export happens only when you start it and goes to the destination you choose through iOS. The developer does not receive a copy.

Notifications

Reminders you enable are scheduled locally by iOS. Their content is not sent to the developer or an advertising service.

Purchases

This is a paid App Store download with a one-time purchase. There is no subscription or in-app purchase. Apple processes the purchase; the developer does not receive payment-card details.

Advertising, analytics, and tracking

TripBee Pro: Trip Planner contains no third-party advertising, analytics, attribution, or tracking SDK. The developer does not build a cross-app or cross-site profile.

Your choices

You control the app data covered by this policy. Delete individual content where the app offers that control, or remove the app to remove its local container. Manage any Apple-hosted copy through Apple settings.

Changes to this policy

If the app changes in a way that affects this policy, this page and its last-updated date will be revised.

Contact

For privacy questions, email hourstag.app@gmail.com.
