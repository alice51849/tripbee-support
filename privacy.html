Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-07-06

TripBee Pro is a fully offline trip planner for iOS. The short version: we do not collect, sell, or share any personal data. There are no accounts, no registration, no login, no ads, no third-party analytics, and no tracking.

1. Data we collect

None. TripBee Pro does not collect personal information. The app does not require an account, sign-up, login, email address, profile, or any connection to our servers. We do not operate analytics or advertising, and we do not use third-party tracking technologies.

2. Content you create stays on your device

TripBee Pro stores the content you create — including trips, daily itinerary items, flights, hotels, restaurants, activities, notes, reminders, packing lists, and photos or ticket attachments such as boarding passes — locally on your device.

Photos are selected through Apple’s system photo picker (PHPicker). The app does not request access to your photo library and does not read or scan your album.

3. No cloud sync, no server

TripBee Pro does not provide cloud sync and does not operate any server for your travel data. Your data remains local to your device. You can manage your own data using the app’s built-in backup/restore features and CSV import.

4. Payment

TripBee Pro is a paid download priced at US$5.99, with a one-time purchase handled entirely by Apple through the App Store. There are no in-app purchases and no subscription. We do not receive your name, card number, billing address, or other payment details.

5. No ads, analytics, or tracking

TripBee Pro contains no advertising, no third-party analytics SDKs, no behavioural profiling, and no cross-app tracking. We do not use the device advertising identifier (IDFA).

6. Data retention and deletion

Because your data is stored locally, you remain in control. Deleting trips or attachments in the app removes those local items. Deleting the app removes its local app data from your device, subject to normal iOS behaviour and any backups you created yourself.

7. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and revise the “Last updated” date above.

8. Contact us

Questions about privacy? Email hourstag.app@gmail.com and we’ll be glad to help.
