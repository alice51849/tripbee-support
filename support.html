How do I create a trip itinerary?

Open TripBee Pro, create a new trip, then add daily items such as flights, hotels, restaurants and activities. Each item can include time, time zone, notes and reminders so your schedule stays clear offline.

Can I attach photos such as boarding passes?

Yes. Add photos or ticket screenshots to itinerary items, such as boarding passes, booking confirmations or receipts. Photos are selected with Apple’s system photo picker and stay stored locally on your device.

How does the smart packing list work?

TripBee Pro can generate a packing list based on trip length, number of travelers and ages. You can edit the list anytime, add custom items, and check everything off before departure.

Does TripBee Pro work offline?

Yes. TripBee Pro is designed to be fully offline. Your itinerary, notes, reminders, attachments and packing lists remain available without an account or server connection.

How do Apple Maps navigation and reminders work?

When an itinerary item has a place or address, tap navigation to open directions in Apple Maps. You can also set reminders for flights, hotel check-in, restaurants and activities.

Can I import, back up or restore my data?

Yes. TripBee Pro supports CSV import plus local backup and restore. These tools help you manage your own trip data while keeping it under your control.

What does the US$5.99 purchase include?

TripBee Pro is a paid App Store download with a one-time purchase. There are no in-app purchases, subscriptions or ads. Apple handles payment, and we do not receive your payment details.

Is my travel data private?

Yes. The app collects no personal data, uses no third-party analytics, contains no ads, does not track you, and does not use IDFA. Your content is stored locally on your device. See our Privacy Policy.

The app feels slow or something does not display correctly.

Make sure you are using the latest version of TripBee Pro and the latest available iOS version for your device. If a problem persists, email us with your device model, iOS version and a short description of the issue.
